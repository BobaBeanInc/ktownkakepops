Our Story

Small batch. Big heart.

K-Town Kake Pops started with a simple idea: make something sweet, make it local, and make it fun. We're based in Kannapolis (K-Town!) and proud of it.

Each cake pop is handcrafted in small batches using quality ingredients. Custom orders are our specialty — whether it's a birthday, baby shower, wedding, or just a Tuesday that needs sweetening up.

We're learning something new with every batch. That's kind of the whole vibe.

How to Order

We don't have a traditional online checkout — every order is personal. Reach out directly and we'll work with you on quantity, flavors, themes, and pickup details.

Orders are accepted on a first-come, first-served basis with advance notice. The more notice the better, especially for large or custom orders!

You can also find our pops already stocked at Boba Bean in Concord — no order needed, just walk in. Check the Kake Pop Locator below for hours and availability.
